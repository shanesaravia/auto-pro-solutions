What We Do

Full-Service Auto Repair

From routine maintenance to complex diagnostics, we've got every corner of your vehicle covered.

Oil Changes & Maintenance

Regular oil changes are the lifeblood of your engine. We use premium oils and perform a comprehensive multi-point inspection with every visit to catch small issues before they become costly repairs.

  • Conventional & synthetic oil
  • Filter replacement
  • Multi-point inspection
  • Fluid top-up
Most Popular

Brake Repair & Service

Your brakes are your most critical safety system. Our certified technicians inspect, repair, and replace brake pads, rotors, calipers, and brake lines — ensuring your vehicle stops the way it should, every time.

  • Brake pad replacement
  • Rotor resurfacing & replacement
  • Brake fluid flush
  • Caliper service

Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Modern vehicles are complex machines. Using advanced diagnostic tools, we accurately identify the root cause of warning lights and performance issues — saving you time, money, and unnecessary repairs.

  • Check engine light diagnosis
  • Computer diagnostics
  • Engine tune-ups
  • Performance optimization

Suspension & Steering

A smooth, controlled ride starts with a properly tuned suspension. We service shocks, struts, tie rods, and steering components to restore handling and comfort to your vehicle.

  • Shock & strut replacement
  • Wheel alignment
  • Tie rod & ball joint service
  • Power steering repair

Electrical & Battery

From dead batteries to complex electrical faults, we diagnose and repair your vehicle's entire electrical system — ensuring reliable starts and proper operation of all electronic components.

  • Battery testing & replacement
  • Alternator & starter repair
  • Electrical fault diagnosis
  • Lighting & fuse service

Seasonal Inspections

Ontario winters are tough on vehicles. Our comprehensive seasonal inspections cover everything from tire condition and antifreeze levels to battery health and belts — keeping you safe all year long.

  • Pre-winter inspection
  • Tire changeover
  • Antifreeze & coolant check
  • MVI-ready inspections
